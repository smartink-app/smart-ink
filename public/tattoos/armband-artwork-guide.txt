SMART INK — WRAP AROUND ARTWORK

Example: continuous-chevron-band.svg / continuous-chevron-band.png
An original geometric band supplied as one complete, flat 360-degree strip.

What to deliver
• A transparent PNG, with only the tattoo artwork. Keep the editable SVG as the master.
• The left edge must continue directly into the right edge, at the same height.
• No empty padding on the left or right; those edges are the join.
• Keep intentional clear space above and below the design inside the image.
• No skin photograph, perspective, studio light, shadow or background.
• Outline lettering and include the complete design; do not send a photo of a band already on an arm.

Size and proportions
The example is 4096 × 640 pixels (6.4:1). That is the same proportion as a band
32 cm around and 5 cm high. Those numbers illustrate the artwork ratio; they
are not measurements of every body in the app.
Prepare your own strip using the intended circumference divided by band height.
The test wraps one copy of the complete strip around the selected arm. To use a
repeating motif, repeat it an integer number of times in the file before export.
Changing the body's proportions or band height can stretch the artwork.

In the studio
Choose “Try a 360° armband” under Tattoo to load this example.
Position moves it along the upper arm; Band height changes its height.
Around the arm turns the design without opening the loop.
“Inspect join” points the camera at the artwork's meeting edge.
Use Replace image to test your own matching-edge PNG in the same wrap mode.
The same band is included in Blender photos.
